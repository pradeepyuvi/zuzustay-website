Easy Onboarding

Get Started in 8 Simple Steps

Start organizing your rental portfolio in less than ten minutes. No setup fees or contracts.

Step 1

Download Naadhe

Get the mobile application on your iOS or Android device from the app stores.

Step 2

Login with Mobile

Input your phone number to initialize authentication securely without complex passwords.

Step 3

Verify via OTP

Confirm the verification code texted to your phone to establish instant access.

Step 4

Create Owner Profile

Set up your business name, logo, contact points, and billing preferences.

Step 5

Add Property

Add PG buildings, hotel structures, or rental houses with location parameters.

Step 6

Add Rooms

Define room configurations, pricing matrices, share rules, and amenities tags.

Step 7

Register Tenants

Add occupancy logs, assign beds to tenants, and set monthly rent dates.

Step 8

Start Managing

Track dues, automate rent alerts, log expenses, and see your business grow.
